🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At AI Prompt Maker (accessible at ai-prompt-maker.onrender.com), the privacy of our visitors is a top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our website and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Prompt Processing

All prompt generation features on AI Prompt Maker run locally inside your browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit the topics, keywords, or generated prompts you enter into our tool.

2. Log Files and Hosting

Like most standard websites, our hosting provider (Render) may automatically record basic server log files such as IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages for security, performance monitoring, and diagnostic purposes.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep this educational tool free for students. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
