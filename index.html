📖 Quick Study Notes: How Prompt Engineering Works

Modern AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude work by predicting the best sequence of words based on the clues you give them. When a prompt is too short—like typing "Explain photosynthesis"—the AI only gives a basic textbook summary.

The 4 Chalkboard Rules for Better Prompts

  • Assign a Specialist Role: Telling the AI to act like a tutor, researcher, or programmer helps it use the right vocabulary and depth.
  • Define Clear Topic Boundaries: Specific keywords keep the AI focused on your exact lesson or problem.
  • Match the Reader Level: Choosing "Students / Beginners" ensures concepts are explained clearly without overwhelming jargon.
  • Control the Output Length: Asking for a quick summary versus an in-depth breakdown saves time when reviewing for exams.
