🏫 About AI Prompt Maker

Welcome to AI Prompt Maker—a free, accessible educational resource and prompt engineering assistant built specifically for students, educators, developers, and everyday learners.

Our Mission

Generative Artificial Intelligence tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude have transformed how students research, debug code, and organize study notes. However, getting accurate, reliable answers requires knowing how to ask the right questions. Our mission is to bridge that gap by providing structured prompt templates and easy-to-follow study guides.

What We Offer

  • Structured Prompt Generator: Quickly convert rough study topics into clear, role-based AI instructions across 10 academic and professional categories.
  • Curated Sample Library: Tested prompt frameworks for literature reviews, code debugging, fact-checking, and student resumes.
  • Practical Study Guides: Beginner-friendly lessons on Zero-Shot, Few-Shot, and Chain-of-Thought prompting techniques.

Privacy-First Design

We believe student tools should be fast, lightweight, and private. Our generator runs 100% locally inside your web browser—meaning we never store, log, or sell the topics or prompts you create.
